\section{An Algebraic Rearrangement}

We begin from the definition of the quantity of interest,

\begin{equation}\tag{1}
y = \frac{a + b}{2}
\end{equation}

where $a$ is the lower endpoint and $b$ is the upper endpoint.

Expanding the fraction over the two terms gives

\begin{equation}\tag{2}
y = \frac{a}{2} + \frac{b}{2}
\end{equation}

which is the same statement written term by term.

\section{A Mistranscribed Line}

Rearranging once more, and making an arithmetic error in the process, we would write

\begin{equation}\tag{3}
y = \frac{a}{2} + \frac{b}{2} + 1
\end{equation}

which does not follow from the line above. This document exists so the pipeline has a step
that must be rejected, not merely left unchecked.
